Put each map in its own folder here:

    Content/VRRealms/Community/Maps/<YourMapName>/Maps/<YourMapName>.umap
    Content/VRRealms/Community/Maps/<YourMapName>/Materials/...
    Content/VRRealms/Community/Maps/<YourMapName>/Meshes/...
    (textures, etc.)

The folder name is your map's identity. The .umap MUST live under a /Maps/
subfolder inside it, exactly as shown. The WorkshopTool detects this layout
automatically and refuses anything outside it.

Do NOT rename "Community" or "Maps" — the running game resolves your content at
/Game/VRRealms/Community/Maps/<YourMapName>/... and the path must match exactly.
