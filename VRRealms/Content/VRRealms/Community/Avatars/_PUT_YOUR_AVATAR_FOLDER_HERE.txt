Put each avatar in its own folder here:

    Content/VRRealms/Community/Avatars/<YourAvatarName>/Meshes/SKM_<YourAvatarName>.uasset
    Content/VRRealms/Community/Avatars/<YourAvatarName>/Materials/...
    Content/VRRealms/Community/Avatars/<YourAvatarName>/Textures/...

When you import your avatar FBX, you MUST set the Skeleton field to the bundled
VR Realms mannequin skeleton at:
    /Game/VRRealms/AvatarRig/SK_VRUE5Mannequin
Do NOT leave it empty — that creates a duplicate skeleton and your avatar's body
will be invisible in game.

Do NOT rename "Community" or "Avatars" — the running game resolves your content at
/Game/VRRealms/Community/Avatars/<YourAvatarName>/... and the path must match exactly.
